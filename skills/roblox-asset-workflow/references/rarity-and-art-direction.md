# Rarity, collection identity and art direction

Use for cosmetic sets, wheels, hats/toppers, skins, boosts and trails. Rarity tiers are **creative guidance**, not Roblox engine limits or a fixed particle budget.

## Three pillars of perceived value

- **Identity:** ownable silhouette, coherent palette, materials, mood and a recognizable detail; avoid recoloring the same generic base for every tier.
- **Exclusivity:** one meaningful signature (form, reaction, motion, texture or sound), not simply higher particle counts.
- **Readability:** recognizable at real match-camera distance and under ordinary game lighting, without concealing the ball, opponents, vehicle cues or camera view. Don't rely on color alone.

## Flexible tier ladder

- **Common:** attractive, complete and varied: clean sport, retro, playful or understated. Never deliberately poor-quality.
- **Rare:** distinctive motif, finish or accent within the collection language.
- **Epic:** stronger silhouette/material treatment, restrained secondary motion if warranted.
- **Legendary:** memorable construction or coordinated gameplay-responsive signature.
- **Mythic:** exceptional design identity, not compulsory neon overload; a minimal but striking form can outperform a particle storm.

Compare **the whole lineup side by side** at gameplay distance. Balance contrast between tiers without making low tiers ugly or all top tiers noisy. Labels and drop rates belong to game economy design, not visual rules.

## Collection and states

- Define a short visual bible: shape vocabulary, palette, focal point, emissive zones, sound mood, allowed variants and shared mount/UV interfaces across wheel, skin, topper, boost and trail.
- Author `Idle` (quiet) → `Move` → `Boost`/`Supersonic` → `Impact` responses **only when the host game exposes those states**. Prefer brief readable reactions to constant emission.
- Preserve gameplay silhouette/hitboxes and competition fairness; offer reduced-flash and low-effect variations if necessary.

## Hero vertical slice

Before multiplying items, finish **one hero cosmetic** end-to-end: concept/reference → model/UV → native VFX/audio if relevant → Studio import → real match-camera check → several equipped players/mobile check when available. Show evidence and record unresolved tests. If it fails identity, readability, safe integration or performance, revise that one item first.
