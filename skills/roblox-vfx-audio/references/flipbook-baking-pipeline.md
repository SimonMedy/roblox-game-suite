# 3D → flipbook bake (Blender / Niagara → Roblox)

**Evidence:** the Blender-side Voidstorm 4×4 RGBA prototype and subsequent SideRush Studio-PC flipbooks were reported by an external agent, not independently reproduced here. A later SideRush tablet 60 Hz trial **reported visible flicker** when eight short `Grid2x2` phases were chained through two `ParticleEmitter`s; a `BillboardGui` replacement and first-shot sharpness/perspective corrections passed reported Play Solo assertions, but their **v103 real-device result remains UNVERIFIED**. Treat the architecture as a candidate to evaluate, not a universal engine guarantee. See [native VFX](vfx-roblox.md).

Use baked camera-facing explosions, impacts and smoke when complex temporal artwork matters more than multi-angle geometry; use native `Beam`/`Trail`/`MeshPart`/emission for reactive or freely viewed effects.

## One hero-effect workflow

1. **Art and motion:** inspect authorized references for timing and silhouette; create original art. Flash/hard cuts, three-tone shading and erosion are style choices, not defaults. Decide camera, one-shot vs loop, and gameplay scale.
2. **Author:** prefer Blender MCP / `bpy` / EEVEE where available; Niagara Flipbook Baker is optional. Keep editable `.blend`, scripts, seeds and settings. Bake/cache simulations when arbitrary frame evaluation would change results.
3. **Render transparent RGBA:** fix camera, lighting, framing and color management; inspect straight and premultiplied alpha on black, white and representative game backgrounds. A controlled `Mix Shader(Transparent BSDF, Emission)` with explicit opacity worked in the reported Voidstorm test; **do not** assume this node recipe handles all additive/Bloom effects. Check color fringes, clipping and glow.
4. **Frames and erosion:** sample deterministically with stable pivots/crop. Inspect occupancy/opacity for **every** frame; evaluate the actual procedural noise range before mapping erosion thresholds (normalize/remap when useful). Skip a warm-up frame only if polluted. For loops, favor a periodic source and test seam continuity; blend ends only if visually correct.
5. **Pack:** resize RGB in **premultiplied-alpha space**, then safely unpremultiply if exporting straight-alpha PNG (avoid dark fringes at translucent edges). Verify zero unintended border alpha and black/white/sky composites. On a 1024² atlas, `Grid2x2` yields four 512² cells, `Grid4x4` 16 cells at 256², `Grid8x8` 64 cells at 128². Choose resolution from **rendered on-screen quad size**, detail and measured client memory, not frame count alone. This example atlas size is **not** a general limit on all Roblox textures.
6. **Studio playback:** import/approve the image and test actual texture availability, `FlipbookLayout`, `FlipbookMode`, alpha, world size, perspective and camera angle. **`OneShot` ignores `FlipbookFramerate`**: playback spans `Lifetime`. For looping/ping-pong, follow documented framerate behavior/caps; sheets are not auto-chained. `FlipbookBlendFrames` blends adjacent sprites, **not physical motion**: test both enabled and disabled at the target cadence. Measure Bloom, overdraw, frame times, texture memory and mobile readability; have a suitable native/reduced fallback.

## High-resolution multi-atlas playback: choose the renderer

- **Several atlases** preserve cell resolution while extending an animation: two 4×4 atlases = 32 frames of 256²; eight 2×2 atlases = 32 frames of 512², assuming 1024² sheets. This costs additional textures/asset availability, not automatically additional on-screen pixels. Preload only what the active quality and cosmetics need. Keep camera, color, crop, phase timing, and a single trigger-specific orientation/pivot continuous across sheets.
- **Few, sufficiently long phases:** sequencing `ParticleEmitter` one-shots can work for a slow, cinematic effect, but check particle lifetime and handoff timing at 30/60 Hz. Do **not** recommend two-emitter ping-pong as the default for many very short phases: a reported SideRush tablet test with eight ~40–60 ms phases and ~4 ms overlap visibly flickered (gaps/double-bright frames). Reusing instances and passing Studio assertions did not prevent the artifact.
- **Short, tightly sequenced phases (candidate):** a pooled world-space `BillboardGui` with preassigned atlas `ImageLabel`s can select **one** active atlas and frame via `ImageRectSize`/`ImageRectOffset`, based on absolute elapsed time (`elapsed / totalDuration`, clamped), without reassigning images on every phase. At each update compute the *global* frame index first, then its atlas index and cell; reset/disable other layers and disconnect idle update callbacks. A 60 Hz client can skip some frames of a 32-frame 0.32 s effect: skipped frames are expected; blank/double frames or rotation jumps are defects. Test image load, multiple rapid triggers, pooled-slot reuse and cleanup. Do not promise zero flicker or negligible cost without real-device evidence.
- **Perspective matters:** `ParticleEmitter.ZOffset` and `BillboardGui.StudsOffset` are **not** interchangeable. A world-space depth shift can alter apparent size; test an off-center camera, perspective, occlusion, orientation and `AlwaysOnTop` behavior. A project may compensate scale from camera distance; verify units and geometry rather than shipping one universal formula.
- **First-use sharpness:** a `PreloadAsync` success is **not** proof that highest-resolution mip levels are permanently resident. Reported SideRush testing found a blurry first `BillboardGui` playback after warming smaller particle visuals. Where relevant, exercise the actual renderer at representative screen size behind a loading/transition screen and verify the **first** visible effect on a device. Near-transparent warmups and forced mip levels are **hypotheses to test**, not guaranteed Roblox API behavior; avoid rendering a costly hidden gallery throughout play. Never claim an exact mip level was loaded without instrumentation.
- **Quality tiers:** an optional SD atlas variant can reduce active texture demands. Ensure inactive HD assets are not unnecessarily preloaded/kept alive in reduced mode; switching quality and equipped effects must not leave stale sprites or callbacks. Count textures and profile resident-memory proxies, latency and hitches rather than claiming fixed VRAM bytes or zero GPU cost.

## PASS / FAIL / UNVERIFIED

- **PASS (Blender only):** reproducible source, frames/sheets with correct alpha/edges, intended timing and QA previews. A single reported example supports a *technique*, not guaranteed shader speed or universal parameters.
- **PASS (Studio):** working client playback, visible size/contrast, frame sequence and transitions, first-use sharpness and repeated triggers at target FPS; include renderer/quality and device in the evidence.
- **FAIL:** black/square fringes, missing or double phase, flicker, blur at first use, rotation snap, broken frame order, wrong scale, unreadable gameplay or bad off-angle flatness; correct and rerender/retest.
- **UNVERIFIED:** unrun Roblox Studio, real-device performance, multiplayer, memory residency or other checks. Source generation and Play Solo assertions alone do not establish mobile PASS.
